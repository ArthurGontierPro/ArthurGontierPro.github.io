About

Since January 2024 I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Glasgow, in the FATA section. I work with Ciaran McCreesh, Matthew McIlree and the VeriPB group on constraint solvers that produce proofs of their reasoning.

Before that I was a PhD student in symmetric cryptanalysis with declarative languages, in the CAPSULE team in Rennes, supervised by Stéphanie Delaune, Patrick Derbez and Charles Prud'homme. I worked on problems such as cube attacks and differential attacks, using several solvers as well as dedicated algorithms.

Earlier, I obtained a Master's degree in Nantes and Brussels focused on optimisation techniques, with CP teachers such as Nicolas Beldiceanu and Eric Monfroy, and did several research internships on multi-objective optimisation, static program analysis and CP explanations.

Research interests

  • Proof logging for constraint solvers. Making solvers justify every inference they make, so that their answers can be checked independently. My current work is on trimming VeriPB proofs to make verification tractable.
  • Constraint programming for cryptanalysis. Modelling very specific cryptographic properties as graph or set constraints for solvers such as Choco and Chuffed. In three years of PhD we published three papers using CP, MILP, SAT and dedicated algorithms.
  • Explanations and conflict analysis. Generating explanations for global constraints from their decompositions, to make explanations easier to use in CP solvers.
  • Generic cryptanalysis tooling. I contributed to TAGADA, a tool where anyone can model a block cipher and have differential cryptanalysis performed on it automatically.
